This article is for IT pros and Teams administrators who want to learn about the spam detection option for Teams Phone Agent.

## Spam detection

Teams Phone Agent automatically identifies and disconnects calls that it determines are spam.

Use a Spam Detection template to override the default behaviour and route spam calls to a different destination.

A Spam Detection template also lets you specify phone numbers that are always treated as spam or never treated as spam.
