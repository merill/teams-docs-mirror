This article is for IT pros and administrators who want to learn how to configure **Holidays** and associated routing for use with Teams Phone Agents and Auto Attendants.

## Holidays

You can use the **Holidays** feature to provide alternate messages and routing for callers when your organization is following a holiday schedule. For example:

- Departments that close or have an adjusted holiday schedule.
- Call queues fronted by an auto attendant that closes or has an adjusted holiday schedule.
- People who are out of office for specific dates and times.

Up to 50 holiday dates and times can be combined into a holiday set. Multiple holiday sets can be applied to a Teams Phone Agent or Auto Attendant.
