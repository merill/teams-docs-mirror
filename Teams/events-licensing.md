# Teams events licensing overview - Microsoft Teams | Microsoft Learn

Important

**Licensing update for Teams Premium and Teams Enterprise**

As of April 1, 2026, some features that were previously only available with Teams Premium are now included with Teams Enterprise. Teams Premium continues to offer advanced meeting protection, advanced communication (like the Queues app), branding and personalization, and intelligence capabilities. [Learn more](https://techcommunity.microsoft.com/blog/microsoftteamsblog/licensing-updates-extend-access-to-advanced-capabilities-in-microsoft-teams-and-/4488312).

Customers who purchased Teams Premium licenses before April 1, 2026 will continue to have access to all the previously included Teams Premium features and product experiences included with these licenses until they expire.

To manage Teams Premium features, your tenant must have at least one active [Teams Premium license](/en-us/microsoftteams/teams-add-on-licensing/licensing-enhance-teams).

This article provides an overview of Microsoft Teams events licensing. It describes Teams events capabilities by license and summarizes the April 1, 2026 licensing changes that moved event features previously included in Teams Premium to Teams Enterprise.

## Teams events capabilities by license

All eligible Teams licenses include the foundational capabilities needed to create and run events, engage attendees, review attendance reports, and publish event recordings.

Event capabilities and attendee capacity vary based on the organizer's license. For Teams events, licenses fall into two categories:

- **Foundational event features**: These licenses provide the foundational capabilities required to create and run events.
- **Advanced event features**: These licenses provide the full Teams events feature set. They include the foundational capabilities plus advanced features such as event workflows, customization, production controls, monitoring, insights, and higher engagement event capacity.

The following table lists licenses based on these categories.

| - | Foundational event features | Advanced event features |
| --- | --- | --- |
| SMB | Microsoft 365 Business Basic, Business Standard, Business Premium | Not applicable |
| Frontline (FLW) | Microsoft 365 F1, F3Office 365 F3 | Not applicable |
| Enterprise | Not applicable | Office 365 E1, E3, E5Microsoft 365 E3, E5, E7 |
| EDU | Office 365 A1, A3 for Students, A5 for StudentsMicrosoft 365 A1, A3 for Students, A5 for Students | Office 365 A3 for Faculty, A5 for FacultyMicrosoft 365 A3 for Faculty, A5 for Faculty |
| Other | Microsoft Teams ExploratoryMicrosoft Teams TrialMicrosoft Teams Essentials | Teams Premium Legacy |

Note

As part of the April 2026 licensing changes, event features that were previously part of Teams Premium moved to Teams Enterprise. Teams Premium licenses purchased after April 2026 don't directly provide access to these capabilities. To access the full set of Teams events features, users must have a license from the Advanced event features category.

Note

Organizers with an active Teams Premium license purchased before April 1, 2026, can continue to host events for up to 100,000 attendees until their current Teams Premium term ends.

After April 1, 2026, when your Teams Premium term expires, events with capabilities to engage with audiences that have more than 3,000 attendees and view-only events with more than 10,000 attendees require an [Attendee Capacity Pack](teams-add-on-licensing/attendee-capacity-pack) sized to the desired attendee capacity. To avoid disruption to large events, plan to purchase the appropriate attendee pack in advance.

## Teams events limits by license

The following table outlines Teams events limits by license.

| - | Foundational event features | Advanced event features |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Number of events | No limit | No limit |
| Maximum attendees for events with registration and full engagement capabilities (attendee microphone and camera, interactive chat, reactions, raise hand, polls, Q&A) | 1,000 | 1,000 |
| Maximum attendees for events with registration and engagement capabilities (chat, reactions, raise hand, polls, Q&A) | 1,000 | 3,000^1^ |
| Maximum attendees for events without registration, in a view-only experience, with Q&A | 10,000 | 10,000 |

^1^To host events with more than 3,000 attendees with engagement capabilities (chat, reactions, raise hand, polls, Q&A), organizations must purchase [Attendee Capacity Pack](teams-add-on-licensing/attendee-capacity-pack) add-on licenses and assign them to organizers. Attendee packs enable events with up to 100,000 attendees.

## Service protection

Microsoft reserves the right to enforce email one-time passcode (eOTP) verification for external event participants to protect service health and guard against fraud or abuse. eOTP enforcement is a service protection measure, not a licensed capability, and isn't tied to a specific SKU tier.
