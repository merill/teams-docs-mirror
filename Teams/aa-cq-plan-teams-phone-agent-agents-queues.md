This article is for IT pros and Teams administrators who want to learn about the **Agents and Queues** option for Teams Phone Agent.

## Agents and Queues

### Microsoft Copilot Studio

With Teams Phone Agent's integration with [Microsoft Copilot Studio (MCS)](/en-us/microsoft-copilot-studio/), organizations can create custom voice agents in MCS to attach to their Teams Phone Agent.

- Create specialized workflows, such as billing, prescription refill, and order status.
- Build in escalation flows that redirect to destinations such as users and call queues by using Tags.

Customers experience a seamless handoff between Teams Phone Agent and the MCS custom voice agent.

MCS custom agents that are published to the Teams Phone channel automatically appear in the **Agents and Queues** list.
