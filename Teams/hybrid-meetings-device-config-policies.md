# Step 6 - Configure meeting policies - Microsoft Teams | Microsoft Learn

![IT Pro audience](media/hybrid-audience-itpro-small.png)

You may need to apply custom bandwidth or meeting policies to this account. For more information, see [Meeting policy settings for audio & video](/en-us/microsoftteams/meeting-policies-audio-and-video). For Teams Rooms, we recommend you set the bandwidth policy no lower than 10 Mbps.

For collaboration purposes, turn on PowerPoint Live, Whiteboard, and shared notes. We recommend you enable the meeting policy setting "Meet now in private meetings" to ensure the "Meet now" functionality on the Teams Rooms console functions. For more information on Teams meeting policies, see [Manage meeting policies in Microsoft Teams](/en-us/microsoftteams/meeting-policies-overview).

Teams Rooms devices include a Net Promoter Score (NPS) survey. This survey can be controlled using Cloud Policy service for Microsoft 365. For more information, see [Manage user feedback in Microsoft Teams](/en-us/microsoftteams/manage-feedback-policies-in-teams).

Teams public preview can be enabled on Teams devices to see Teams features earlier than general availability. For more information, see [Microsoft Teams Public preview](/en-us/microsoftteams/public-preview-doc-updates) on how to enable public preview.
