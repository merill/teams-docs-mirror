# Facilitator in Teams Rooms - Microsoft Teams | Microsoft Learn

Facilitator brings AI-powered experiences to Teams Rooms during scheduled, hybrid, and in-person meetings.

The Facilitator agent can be used in:

- **Facilitator in Teams:** Used for Teams meetings that are scheduled on a calendar. This can include a Teams Rooms device, where Facilitator chat interactions and AI notes can be viewed by in-room participants.
- **Facilitator in Teams Rooms:** Used for unscheduled in-person discussions with a Teams Room device available in the meeting room. Facilitator can serve as the note taker by capturing discussion points and action items for these meetings.

## Facilitator in Teams for scheduled meetings

Facilitator is an AI-powered agent in Teams that can automatically take notes of the conversations that happen during a meeting, provide key highlights, and answer prompts in the meeting chat. If Teams Rooms is added to the scheduled meeting, in-room participants can view Facilitator interactions in the **Chat** panel, navigate notes taken during the meeting in the **Notes** panel, and track timer for allocated agenda topics. This is a consumption-only experience for in-room participants.

Only the meeting organizer requires a Copilot license. For more information and the requirements, see [Set up Facilitator in Microsoft Teams for collaborative AI-generated notes](/en-us/microsoftteams/facilitator-teams).

### Experience

In a scheduled meeting with Teams Rooms devices invited, you can view notes generated by Facilitator by tapping the **Notes** button.

![Screenshot that shows the notes button on the console that will open/close the notes panel on the front-of-room display.](media/facilitator-teams-rooms/notes-button-on-ubar.png)*Notes button on the console to open and close the Notes panel.*

![Screenshot that shows the notes panel on the front-of-room display in a meeting.](media/facilitator-teams-rooms/agenda-timer-front-of-room-display.png)*Notes panel on the front-of-room display*

Facilitator can proactively share insights, reminders and time left in a meeting, as well as respond to participant questions or commands in the **Chat** panel.

![Screenshot that shows Facilitator messages in chat panel on the front-of-room display.](media/facilitator-teams-rooms/agenda-timer-front-of-room-display.png)*Messages from Facilitator in the Chat panel on the front-of-room display*

If there is an agenda in the meeting invite or agenda added to the Loop meeting notes before the meeting, agenda timer will appear at the front-of-room display to show allocation of time for each topic.

## Facilitator in Teams Rooms

Facilitator in Teams Rooms can create rich AI notes with summaries and action items that include speaker attribution for unscheduled meetings on a Teams Rooms device, similar to the in-meeting experience. Facilitator in Teams Rooms can be initiated by tapping on the “Take notes” button on the home screen of the console or touch board.

### Set up Facilitator for AI-generated notes

Note

Facilitator in Teams Rooms for in-person note-taking is in preview and is currently included with Teams Rooms Pro. Preview features might not be complete, and the experience and licensing model may change before final release.

To enable AI-generated notes using Facilitator in Teams Rooms:

**Pre-requisite: Assign a Teams Rooms Pro license to each resource account you have for your Teams Rooms devices**

For more information, see [Microsoft Teams Rooms licenses](/en-us/microsoftteams/rooms/rooms-licensing).

**Step 1: Turn on Facilitator in Teams admin center**

Enable Facilitator app for the room resource account in the Teams admin center. If app availability is set to Everyone, the room account is included. If availability is scoped to People and Groups, include the Teams Rooms resource account.

**Step 2: Turn on transcription for meetings**

For more information, see [Manage transcription for meetings](/en-us/microsoftteams/meeting-transcription-captions).

**Step 3: Turn on Loop experiences**

To enable Loop for the room account, see [Manage Loop components in your organization](/en-us/microsoft-365/loop/loop-components-configuration). Facilitator stores AI-generated notes as a Loop file in SharePoint Embedded storage that belongs to the tenant and users who saved the notes can access it later. The data is treated as meeting transcript data.

**Step 4: Turn on note-taking for Teams Rooms**

In Pro Management portal, select **Rooms** from the left navigation &gt; select your desired room &gt; **Settings** &gt; **Device** &gt; **Show Take notes button on the home screen.** The default setting is enabled.

### Experience

After you complete the required steps and meet the prerequisites, in-room participants will see a Facilitator icon on the front-of-room display and a “Tap to take notes” entry point on the console.

![Screenshot that shows the Takes Notes message on the front-of-room display.](media/facilitator-teams-rooms/take-notes-front-of-room.png)*"Take notes with Facilitator" on the front-of-room display*

![Screenshot that shows the Take Notes message on the console.](media/facilitator-teams-rooms/room-console-homescreen.png)*“Tap to take notes" entry point on console*

Notes can take a few minutes to generate and appear on the front-of-room display as participants speak. At the end of the session, select **Save notes** and ensure participants’ names are added in the dialog pop-up so they can access the notes and transcript later on their personal devices. Unsaved notes are deleted after the session ends.

## Accessing notes

To access notes after the meeting, navigate to the Teams meeting chat titled “Meeting with [conference room name].” At the top of the meeting chat, select the **Recap** tab, and then select **Notes** to find the AI-generated notes and transcript.

![Screenshot that shows the recap notes page on Teams desktop.](media/facilitator-teams-rooms/desktop-recap-notes.png)*Teams desktop view of notes in Recap tab*

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is this feature available in both Teams Rooms on Windows and Android?**

> 
> Facilitator is available on Teams Rooms on Windows and is projected to release on Teams Rooms on Android in a future release.

**Where do I access the notes after the meeting?**

> 
> On your Teams calendar, navigate to the meeting &gt; **Recap** &gt; **Notes**.

**Can I turn off Facilitator during the discussion?**

> 
> You can mute the microphone in the room to ensure Facilitator or transcription doesn't capture any content. You can also remove Facilitator during a scheduled meeting from the Participant panel.

**Is Facilitator recording the meeting?**

> 
> Facilitator only turns on transcription during the meeting. The meeting won't be recorded.

**How are the meeting room participants identified and attributed in AI notes and transcription?**

> 
> Currently, only participants who add their names at the beginning of the meeting session will be identified and attributed, provided that they have enrolled their profiles.
