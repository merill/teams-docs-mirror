# Plan your upgrade from Skype for Business to Microsoft Teams

Microsoft Teams is designed to help people stay connected, collaborate seamlessly, and simplify work in a secure and compliant way - all in one place. Here you’ll find guidance, training, and resources to implement a successful upgrade from Skype for Business to Teams.

## Resources for planning a successful Teams upgrade and beyond

A healthy technical environment, sustained user adoption, and the realization of your business goals all begin with proper planning.

![](/en-us/office/media/icons/walkthrough-map-teams.svg)[Ignite Session: Design your path to Teams](https://go.microsoft.com/fwlink/?linkid=2154668)
This session from Ignite 2020 helps you find the approach most suitable for your Skype for Business implementation and goals.

![](/en-us/office/media/icons/success-teams.svg)[Upgrade Success Kit](https://aka.ms/UpgradeSuccessKit)
Download user readiness materials including communications templates, user surveys, and sample project and pilot test plans.

![](/en-us/office/media/icons/document-teams.svg)[Teams admin documentation](./)
Learn how to roll out and manage Teams, and prepare your users for new communications and collaboration experiences.

![](/en-us/office/media/icons/presenter-with-podium.svg)[Teams Instructor-led training](https://aka.ms/teamslivetraining)
Free, live, online training classes designed to get you up and running with Teams.

![](/en-us/office/media/icons/migration-teams.svg)[Skype for Business Online retirement](skype-for-business-online-retirement)
Prepare for the July 31, 2021 retirement of the Skype for Business Online service and learn how to plan a successful transition to Teams.

![](/en-us/office/media/icons/blog-site-teams.svg)[Microsoft Teams blog](https://techcommunity.microsoft.com/t5/microsoft-teams-blog/bg-p/MicrosoftTeamsBlog)
Learn best practices, news, and trends directly from the team behind Microsoft Teams.

![](/en-us/office/media/icons/trend-graph-teams.svg)[Total Economic Impact of Teams](https://www.microsoft.com/microsoft-365/blog/wp-content/uploads/sites/2/2019/04/Total-Economic-Impact-Microsoft-Teams.pdf)
Dive into the Forrester Research report that quantifies improvements in employee and company performance after adopting Teams.
