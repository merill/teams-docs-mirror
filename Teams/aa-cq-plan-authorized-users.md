# Planning - Authorized users for voice applications - Microsoft Teams | Microsoft Learn

This article is for IT pros and Teams administrators who want to delegate certain Teams Phone Agent, Auto Attendant, or Call Queue configuration capabilities to the users in their organization.

## Authorized users

Through **Authorized users**, Teams administrators can enable users in the organization to manage day-to-day operational changes directly. **Authorized users** don't require access to the [Teams admin center](https://go.microsoft.com/fwlink/p/?linkid=2066851), nor do they require a [Teams Administrator role](using-admin-roles).

Based on the permissions granted to the authorized user, they can configure business, after hours, and holiday greetings on Teams Phone Agents, Auto Attendants, and Call Queues.

In addition, with [Queues App](manage-queues-app), authorized users can:

- Configure call routing for business hours, after hours, and holidays.
- Configure call queue membership, routing method, presence-based routing, and exception handling.
- Access real-time and historical reporting.

Historical reporting is also available to authorized users with the [Teams Phone Agent, Auto Attendant and Call Queue historical reports](aa-cq-report-historical) in Power BI. No extra licensing is required.

For a complete list of features, see [Manage voice applications policies](aa-cq-manage-voice-applications-policies).
