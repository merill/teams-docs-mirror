# Set up calendar syncing between Google Workspace and Microsoft Teams - Microsoft Teams | Microsoft Learn

Important

Calendar syncing between Google Workspace and Microsoft Teams will no longer be supported beginning October 2026.

## What is changing?

Beginning October 2026, calendar syncing between Google Workspace and Microsoft Teams will no longer be supported. Calendars that were previously setup for syncing will no longer sync. Admins will no longer be able to manage their custom domain or calendar sync from the Admin app within Teams.

Admins can continue to use Microsoft Admin Center to manage their custom domain.

## How should I prepare for this change?

1. Notify their end users that calendars will no longer sync.
2. Retrieve any account matching information that they would like to reference from the Admin app in Teams
3. End users who wish to schedule Microsoft Teams meetings from their Google Calendar can use the [Microsoft Teams Meeting add-on for Google Workspace.](https://workspace.google.com/u/3/marketplace/app/microsoft_teams_meeting/961264221134)
