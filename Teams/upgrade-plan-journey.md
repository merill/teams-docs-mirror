# Plan for your Skype for Business to Microsoft Teams upgrade - Microsoft Teams | Microsoft Learn

![Diagram of the Teams upgrade journey.](media/upgrade-banner-main.png)

A healthy technical environment, sustained user adoption, and the realization of your business goals all begin with proper planning. A solid plan gives you a framework for your upgrade project and ensures that all stakeholders are working toward the same goals. By taking time to properly define success, you can measure results as you progress through your deployment and verify that you're achieving the outcomes you wanted. Planning your journey includes:

- [Enlisting your project stakeholders](upgrade-enlist-stakeholders)
- [Defining the scope of your project](upgrade-define-project-scope)
- [Understanding coexistence and interoperability of Skype for Business and Teams](teams-and-skypeforbusiness-coexistence-and-interoperability)
- [Choosing your upgrade journey based on your business needs](upgrade-and-coexistence-of-skypeforbusiness-and-teams)
- [Planning a user pilot](pilot-essentials)

After you've planned your journey, you will read topics about [preparing your environment](upgrade-prepare-environment) and [preparing your organization](upgrade-prepare-organization) before you [implement your upgrade](upgrade-to-teams).

Join the [Plan your upgrade](upgrade-workshops-landing-page) session first to get started.
