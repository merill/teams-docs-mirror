# Get started driving adoption of Microsoft Teams - Microsoft Teams | Microsoft Learn

By driving adoption of Microsoft Teams, you can deliver an excellent user experience and increased business value inside your company. Our goal is to get you up and running quickly so that you can begin your journey with Teams. This guide focuses on user adoption of Teams and has dependencies on your environment's technical readiness, which is addressed in other sections of our documentation.

## Adoption prerequisites

Before getting started, make sure that:

- [Microsoft Teams client](teams-client-desktop-admin) is installed and [licenses are assigned](/en-us/office365/servicedescriptions/teams-service-description) in your environment. A minimum license should be assigned to your core team and to the early adopters who participates in this initial project phase.
- You've downloaded the desktop and mobile applications.

## Adoption framework

Microsoft 365 and Office 365 have an adoption framework that applies to major workloads in the service. This framework addresses the core steps that any organization should take to support cloud services. To learn more about the overall adoption framework, see [Microsoft Enterprise documentation and resources](/en-us/microsoft-365/enterprise/).

In this Teams adoption plan, we've organized the steps in phases and provided specific Teams guidance as outlined in Adoption phases, next.

## Adoption phases

Every adoption project varies in size and complexity based on your environment, but the initial steps are identical across the board. We believe that breaking down the process into three distinct phases—startup, experiment, and enable—can streamline adoption for everyone.

- **Start** - In this first phase, gather your team together, set up your initial teams, and use Teams to begin planning Teams adoption. This approach increases your technical familiarity with the product and help you to build the skills necessary to successfully complete subsequent phases.
- **Experiment** - In this phase of controlled growth, bring your internal champions and early adopters onboard. Speak with your business users to identify scenarios that would immediately benefit from the collaboration and communications capabilities that Teams provides. Gather feedback that informs your broad-scale adoption phase, and make service decisions about governance and lifecycle management that ensures a successful deployment.
- **Scale** - This phase is the broad-scale deployment phase in which you turn on Teams capabilities for all your employees. In this phase, the size of your organization determines whether this phase is a "go big" project that turns on Teams for all employees simultaneously or if you approach this phase by region, business unit, or other method of segmenting your employee population. In this phase you move to a continuous delivery model. Employees, leaders, and business units want to expand their use of Teams and require training and your engagement to understand how to best use Teams and other features of Microsoft 365 or Office 365.

Each of these adoption phases depends on your technical and environmental readiness for Teams. Let's get started!
