# Country/region availability - Audio Conferencing, Calling Plans - Microsoft Teams | Microsoft Learn

You can select a country or region to see what cloud voice features are available to your organization.

Note

Availability doesn't indicate whether Know Your Customer (KYC) verification is required. Before acquiring or porting telephone numbers, verify whether KYC applies for the selected country or region and obtain KYC approval. KYC review is completed at the tenant level, and most reviews are completed within three business days. Complete KYC early so that number acquisition or porting isn't delayed.

## Want to learn more about these services

### Audio Conferencing

Sometimes people in your organization will need to use a phone to call in to a meeting. Teams includes the Audio-Conferencing feature for just this situation! People can call in to meetings using a phone instead of using the Teams apps on a mobile device or PC. When you've decided that you want Audio Conferencing for your organization, buy one **Audio Conferencing** license for each person in your organization who is going to schedule/host an audio meeting.

With Audio Conferencing, there are toll and toll-free phone numbers that your users can use to dial in to meetings. Toll numbers from several countries or regions are automatically assigned as shared audio-conferencing numbers to organizations when they're enabled for Audio Conferencing. Dedicated toll and toll-free numbers can be assigned to your organization from additional cities.

Important

There isn't a resource that contains a listing of all of the dial-in numbers for Audio Conferencing. To find out if there are dial-in phone numbers available for your country/region you can use the drop-down button at the top of this article to select your country and then refer the tables under "Audio Conferencing" and "Toll and Toll-free numbers for services" to find out what phone numbers are available for your country. If you are looking for phone number from a specific city/area code, you can submit a request for "phone number support". To do this go to **Teams admin center** &gt; **Voice** &gt; **Phone Numbers**, click **Add**, and then click "Get phone number support" at the top right-hand corner of the page. On the resulting page you can create and submit a ticket for our phone number support teams. Please note that we may not be able to provide phone numbers for every city or area code within your country or region.

Use the drop-down list at the top of the page to search for a country or region and find out if Audio Conferencing is available for purchase in your country/region.

To learn more about Audio Conferencing, see

[Audio Conferencing common questions](/en-us/MicrosoftTeams/audio-conferencing-common-questions)

[Plan Audio Conferencing for Microsoft Teams](/en-us/microsoftteams/audio-conferencing-in-office-365)

### Teams Phone

With Teams Phone, you can create auto attendants and call queues (with a toll or toll-free number) to answer incoming calls for your organization. If you add a Calling Plan for users in addition to Teams phone, they can use Teams to take care of basic call-control tasks, such as placing and receiving calls to telephone numbers, transferring calls, and muting and unmuting calls. Teams Phone users can also click a name in their address book, and Teams will place a call to that person. To place and receive calls, Teams Phone users use their mobile devices, a headset with a laptop or PC, or one of many IP phones that work with Teams.

For more information on Teams phone see.

[What is Teams Phone](/en-us/microsoftteams/what-is-phone-system-in-office-365).

[Pricing for Teams Phone](https://www.microsoft.com/microsoft-teams/voice-calling?#office-StandaloneSKU-pkdogb9)

### Microsoft Teams Calling Plans

Along with Teams Phone, Calling Plans let users make and receive phone calls. You first get a user (subscriber) phone number (not a service number) to assign to the user and then assign a Calling Plan. There are three types of Calling Plans available: **Domestic**, **International**, and **Pay-As-You-Go**. For more information, see [Calling Plans for Microsoft Teams](/en-us/MicrosoftTeams/calling-plans-for-office-365).

You can also see [Microsoft Teams add-on licensing options](/en-us/MicrosoftTeams/teams-add-on-licensing/microsoft-teams-add-on-licensing) for licensing information.

Use the drop-down list at the top of the page to search for a country or region and find out if Calling Plans are available for purchase for your country/region.

Note

The country/region is based on the location of the user's license in the **Microsoft 365 admin center** &gt; **Active Users** and **not** the billing address listed under the **Organization Profile** in the **Microsoft 365 admin center**.

### Toll and toll-free phone numbers for services

In addition to getting phone numbers for individual users, it's also possible to search and acquire toll or toll-free phone numbers for services such as Audio Conferencing (for conference bridges), auto attendants, and call queues. These numbers are called service numbers. The availability of toll phone numbers from some of these locations might vary at any given time depending on inventory levels.

If you want to get a toll or toll-free service number, you can see [Get new phone numbers](/en-us/microsoftteams/getting-phone-numbers-for-your-users). If you already have a toll or toll-free service number that you want to transfer/port from a different provider, see [Planning to transfer telephone numbers to Teams](/en-us/microsoftteams/phone-number-calling-plans/port-order-overview).

Use the drop-down list at the top of the page to search for a country or region and find out if Toll and Toll-free phone numbers are available for your country/region.

Note

The availability of toll phone numbers from some of these locations might vary at any given time depending on inventory levels.

### Communications Credits

Communication Credits are a billing tool to pay for consumed minutes of Audio Conferencing and Microsoft Calling Plan overages. Outbound calls to some destinations may already be included in your Audio Conferencing or Calling Plan subscription. These subscriptions may have limits on total number of minutes available for usage. If you exceed these limits and run out of minutes for your organization (depending on your Calling Plan or Audio-Conferencing plan or your country/region), your users won't be able to make calls or dial out from meetings, then you would need a mechanism to allow you to pay for the additional usage. Communication credits can help in this situation.

We recommend that you set up Communications Credits for your Audio Conferencing and Calling Plans users that need the ability to dial out to **any destination**. Outbound calls to many countries/regions are included, but some destinations might not be included in your Calling Plan or Audio-Conferencing subscriptions. Communication credits would allow your users to place outbound calls to destinations/countries that are not included in your Calling Plan or Audio-Conferencing subscriptions.

Use the drop-down list at the top of the page to search for a country or region and find out if Communication credits are available for purchase in your country/region.

To learn more about Communication credits see [What are Communications Credits?](/en-us/MicrosoftTeams/what-are-communications-credits)
